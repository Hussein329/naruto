The 21-day build & launch plan

1.5–3 hours a day. Buffer days on 7 & 14. Everything live on Day 21. Sequence-dependent, not clock-dependent.

  1. 01
    Build Tier 1 core. Habit database + 7-day checklist.
  2. 02
    Finish Tier 1 styling. Weekly summary view, then duplicate-test it.
  3. 03
    List Tier 1. Write the listing, mock a thumbnail, publish to Etsy/Gumroad/Notion marketplace.
  4. 04
    Start Tier 2. Daily Log tab + checkbox habit columns.
  5. 05
    Build the Dashboard. Line chart + bar chart.
  6. 06
    Correlation section. Mood/energy vs. completion, plus category tagging.
  7. 07
    Buffer day. Catch up on overflow, answer early Tier 1 buyer questions.
  1. 08
    Monthly Review tab. Auto-pull formulas for top/bottom habits.
  2. 09
    Full styling pass. Colours, fonts, protect the formula cells.
  3. 10
    QA + launch Tier 2. Fresh duplicate test, fix breakages, publish the listing.
  4. 11
    Tier 3 foundations. Character Sheet + Daily Quests database.
  5. 12
    XP system. XP rollup, Level formula, progress bar.
  6. 13
    Goal Map. Relations/rollups linking quests → milestones → Boss Goals.
  7. 14
    Buffer day.
  1. 15
    Time-Block Planner. Calendar/timeline view for habit time slots.
  2. 16
    Icon pack. Source or design it, apply across the Tier 3 dashboard.
  3. 17
    Badge gallery. XP-threshold reveal logic.
  4. 18
    Write the guidebook / script the video.
  5. 19
    Finish the guide or video. Export as PDF or upload.
  6. 20
    Full QA pass. Fresh duplicate test — click every link, relation, and rollup.
  7. 21
    Launch. Write the Tier 3 listing, publish, cross-link all three tiers for upsells.

After launch — the daily 15–30 min routine

  • Same-day support. Answer buyer questions the day they come in.
  • Follow the sales. Whichever tier sells best, double down there next.
  • Weekly review. Read reviews once a week, log feature requests for v2.

What we don't over-invest in

  • No real animated app for Tier 3 — Notion + good formulas already reads as "gamified."
  • No hand-built Excel version of Tier 2 until Tier 1 proves people are buying.
  • No skipping the fresh-duplicate test — it's the #1 source of refund requests.

What every tier gets, no exceptions

  • Duplicated into a blank workspace and clicked through, end to end.
  • One consistent visual identity, not a grab-bag of colours.
  • A delivery mechanism that works the moment a buyer clicks it.
